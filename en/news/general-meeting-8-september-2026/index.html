General meeting of the Estonian Apnea Sports League, 8 September 2026

The meeting admitted new members: the Estonian Apnea Sports League (AIDA Estonia) is now made up of five sports clubs.

The meeting admitted new members. The membership of the Estonian Apnea Sports League (AIDA Estonia) now consists of five sports clubs:

  1. Spordiklubi Meregrupp (Harju County)
  2. Spordiklubi AquaStar (Paldiski)
  3. Spordikool Akvalang (Tallinn)
  4. Academy Swim MB (Tallinn)
  5. Spordiklubi Yongsheng (Tallinn)
