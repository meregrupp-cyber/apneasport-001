The AIDA Estonia journey has begun

An address by Taniel Suvi, President of the Estonian Apnea Sports League, on joining AIDA: the trust we have been given is now ours to carry, and the doors of AIDA Estonia are open to everyone.

Taniel Suvi

Dear friends, divers and supporters!

Today is an important day for Estonian freediving. Estonia is officially a member of AIDA (see observers).

As Tomas Rudžinskis, one of the strongest supporters of the accession process, commented:

We got trust of one of the most reputable Freediving associations in World. We need to proof this trust.

That trust is now ours to carry.

This did not happen by itself. My sincere thanks to everyone who worked towards it, and to those who helped quietly in the background. Tomas also referred to a beautiful thought that Zsófia shared right after her world record:

Great Team is nothing without Great People joining to support the great team.

A great team is nothing without great people joining to support it. That is exactly what we want to build on. The doors of AIDA Estonia are open to everyone who loves water and depth: to beginners, experienced divers, coaches and anyone who wants to contribute. Everyone’s experience and ideas make us stronger.

Freediving is one of the fastest growing sports in the world. Let us bring that same momentum to Estonian freediving: learning from each other, sharing knowledge and growing together.

All official AIDA Estonia information will in future be available at apneasport.ee and on the EAPSL Facebook page.

Thank you, and let us take a deep breath: the AIDA Estonia journey has begun!

Taniel Suvi
President of the Estonian Apnea Sports League
