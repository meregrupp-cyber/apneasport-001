What is EAPSL?

A shared space for skill, safety and progress.

We create safe, fair and accessible conditions in Estonia for practising, training, competing and representing apnea sports internationally.

Explore our mission→

Three sports

Many worlds below one surface.

Each discipline has its own technique and character. Skill, control and deliberate safety connect them all.

01

Freediving

Freediving combines breath control, efficient movement and deliberate safety in the pool and open water.

Sport grows in clubs.
