Freediving

Freediving combines breath control, efficient movement and deliberate safety in the pool and open water.

On this page

What is it?

Freediving is underwater activity on a single breath, without carrying breathing gas.

EAPSL acts as AIDA Estonia in freediving. This role does not extend in the same way to the League’s other sports.

Who is it for?

  • New participants who want a calm, supervised introduction.
  • Recreational and competitive athletes developing technique systematically.

How to begin

  1. Choose a competent instructor or recognised education pathway.
  2. Learn breathing, buddy and rescue principles before pursuing depth.
  3. Never practise breath-holding in water alone.

Core disciplines

  • Static apnea
  • Dynamic apnea
  • Pool and open-water disciplines

Safety

  • Competent supervision and a buddy system
  • Loads appropriate to current skills
  • Rescue readiness and incident reporting

Training and qualifications

Current requirements of the AIDA education and certification system are published through AIDA International's official channels.

Competition

EAPSL freediving competitions, record attempts and safety standards follow current AIDA International rules and Estonian law.
