The meeting admitted new members. The membership of the Estonian Apnea Sports League
(AIDA Estonia) now consists of [five sports clubs](/en/#liikmesklubid):

1. Spordiklubi Meregrupp (Harju County)
2. Spordiklubi AquaStar (Paldiski)
3. Spordikool Akvalang (Tallinn)
4. Academy Swim MB (Tallinn)
5. Spordiklubi Yongsheng (Tallinn)

The meeting also decided to open talks with the
[Estonian Olympic Committee](https://www.eok.ee/en) on joining the committee and on the
professional qualification of coaches (EKR, the Estonian Qualifications Framework).
